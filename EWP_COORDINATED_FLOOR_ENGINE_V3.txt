CadTech EWP Coordinated Floor Engine v3

Added to the existing Roof/Truss + EWP Floor Framing PRO package:
- Regenerates joist lines from the floor boundary and current spacing.
- Cuts/segments joists at rectangular floor openings instead of leaving full joists through voids.
- Creates paired LVL opening headers and doubled trimmer records around each opening.
- Keeps four perimeter rim-board members as explicit framing records.
- Rebuilds framingMembers as one coordinated floor assembly.
- Adds cut-joist/opening-frame regeneration audit data.
- Adds framing geometry CSV export with axis, position, start/end, span and ply data.
- Existing roof/truss, wall, EWP schedules, whole-building coordination and load-path tools are retained.

Engineering note: manufacturer-specific EWP span, hole, bearing, hanger and connection capacities remain DESIGN REQUIRED until validated product data is loaded.
