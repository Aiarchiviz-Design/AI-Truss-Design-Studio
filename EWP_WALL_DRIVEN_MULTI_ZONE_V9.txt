CadTech EWP V9 — Outer + Inner Wall Driven Multi-Zone Floor Framing

- Exterior/outer walls define the floor envelope and perimeter supports.
- Interior walls are recognized separately and treated as candidate bearing/support lines.
- Layers containing NON-BEARING, NONBEARING, PARTITION or NBR are not automatically used as bearings.
- Wall/support geometry divides the floor into framing zones.
- Joist direction is selected from support-to-support zone spans unless manually overridden.
- Wall-zone CSV records support boundaries and review status.
- Interior bearing classification remains REVIEW until confirmed by project structural information.
- Manufacturer-specific EWP capacities remain DESIGN REQUIRED without validated product data.
