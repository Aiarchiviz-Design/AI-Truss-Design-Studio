CadTech EWP Product + Service Coordination V5
- Retains all V4 roof/truss, wall, whole-building and EWP floor features.
- Adds EWP product-library framework with explicit validated/unvalidated state.
- Adds member-level span, bearing and hole-rule check records.
- Adds service/hole coordination engine for penetrations crossing I-joist members.
- Adds Product Checks CSV and Service Checks CSV exports.
- Generic bundled product records are geometry-only and do not provide structural capacities.
- PASS/FAIL is only enabled when a validated product dataset contains the required limits/rules; otherwise DESIGN REQUIRED is preserved.
