CadTech Truss Joint Fix V9.8  (overhang alignment + deep-rafter heels)
======================================================================
Point 1 - some Common / Girder trusses still had unfixed heel connections
  Cause: the heel stub only exists in the clear space between the bottom-chord top face and the rafter underside.
  After "Analyze", Common/Girder trusses get deep top chords (2x10 / 2x12). With the 250 mm heel that clear
  space shrinks to a sliver (~75 mm at 2x10, nothing at 2x12) and the old code flipped between two different joint
  modes at an arbitrary depth, leaving a short "hook" stub that did not seat on the rafter.
  Fix (app/connection-geometry.js): if the clear space is under 100 mm the stub is dropped and the bottom chord is
  sawn flush to the rafter underside (classic heel). Otherwise the seated stub from V9.7 is unchanged.
  Slivers (< 50 mm mean length) left between two faces are no longer drawn as timbers.
  Note: a heel with less than 100 mm clear is now always a sawn heel. To keep a stub on a deep rafter, raise the Heel depth.

Point 2 - top of the top chord must match the top of the overhang
  Cause: Analyze resized the 'top' members but never the 'overhang' member, so the overhang stayed 2x4 while the
  rafter became e.g. 2x6. Both are centred on the same line, so the overhang top sat lower (25 mm step for 2x6).
  Fix: (a) advanced-ui.js - overhang takes the top-chord section after analysis;
       (b) connection-geometry.js - the overhang solid always takes the depth and thickness of the top chord it
           continues, so existing / edited models are corrected too. Top faces are now one continuous line.

Tests: node app/test-joint-solids-v98.mjs (new). test-heel-joints.mjs now uses a 300 mm heel for its 150 mm rafter.
Cache-busting query strings bumped (connection-geometry v12, advanced-ui v5) so the browser loads the new code.
Geometry only - not connector-plate design.
