CadTech Truss Reference Geometry V9.4
=====================================
Visual target derived from the user-supplied common, hip/step-down, mono and attic truss images:
- Top and bottom chords define the controlling truss envelope.
- Collinear chord segments remain continuous through panel points.
- Webs fit between chord faces; they do not pass through chords.
- Diagonal web ends use face-matching oblique cuts.
- Verticals/king posts seat between chord faces.
- Both top chords use a common apex miter/cut plane at the peak.
- At a heel the sloping top chord continues into the intended eave overhang while the bottom chord terminates at the heel joint.
- Attic-room chords and piggyback/step-down members follow the same face-to-face fitting rules.
- Analytical panel nodes are retained for analysis; physical timber solids are generated from joint cut planes.
- Truss-to-truss receiver clipping remains separate from internal member fabrication cuts.
- OBJ export uses the same physical joint-cut geometry as the 3D viewport.
Connector plate engineering is not inferred from these geometry rules.
